How to Plan a Cycling Holiday in Mallorca with AI

24 November 2026 · 11 min read

You have four days in Mallorca. You want to ride to the lighthouse at Cap de Formentor, stop for photos and still make it back for lunch with your family. Ask ChatGPT for a scenic ride in Mallorca and you might get a list that includes Formentor, Sóller and Sa Calobra. All three sound tempting. Which one fits into your morning?

At Venga Cycling Mallorca, we’d start with two questions. Where are you riding from, and what time do you want to be back? With those answers, you can use AI to plan a holiday around the riding you enjoy. Here’s how we’d do it for a four-day stay in Port de Pollença, with three rides, a morning off the bike and five prompts you can copy into ChatGPT.

What should you tell AI about the way you ride?

Describe a ride you regularly do at home. “I usually ride 60 km on flat roads, but I rarely descend on tight hairpins” tells an assistant more than “I’m pretty fit”. If two of you are travelling, describe both riders. One of you might want a long climb while the other is looking forward to coffee by the sea.

Give your exact starting point. Pollença and Port de Pollença are different places, and choosing the wrong one changes your distance before you’ve even left the hotel. Add your dates, bike type, preferred surface and the time you need to be back. If you have a morning free from 9 am until noon and want half an hour for coffee, you have at most two and a half hours on the bike.

Try this prompt when you start planning

We’re spending four days in Port de Pollença from [arrival date] to [departure date]. We ride road bikes. I usually ride 70 km, and my partner is comfortable with 40 km on flat roads. We’d like to ride together, see the coast and try one longer climb. We have two mornings with three hours available and one with no fixed return time. Suggest three rides and one morning off the bike. For each ride, give us a destination, a place to turn back and time for stops. Include a source for each distance and tell us whether it covers the return trip.

Can you ride to the Formentor lighthouse and get back for lunch?

First, decide what you mean by “riding to Formentor”. You can turn back at the Mirador es Colomer viewpoint, continue to Platja de Formentor beach or ride all the way to the lighthouse. If the lighthouse is the place you’ve seen in the photos, enter Far de Formentor in your route planner. Then add the return leg to your hotel.

Pollença’s official cycling guide gives a distance of 40.5 km for the round trip from Port de Pollença to the lighthouse via Platja de Formentor. It rates the ride as difficult. If 40 km sounds like a quick spin after work, take a look at the profile. On the way back, some of the descents you enjoyed on the way out become climbs. Official cycling route to Cap de Formentor

Say you leave at 8.30 am and want to be back at the hotel by noon. That gives you three and a half hours. Allow 25 minutes for photos and 20 minutes of spare time, and you have 2 hours and 45 minutes of riding time. Compare that with your usual pace on similar terrain. If reaching the lighthouse would use every minute, Mirador es Colomer gives you a shorter ride with time to enjoy the view.

Try this prompt to compare the two rides

I’ve saved two routes from my hotel in Port de Pollença. One goes to Mirador es Colomer and back. The other goes to the Formentor lighthouse and back. Here are the distance, elevation gain and estimated riding time for each: [paste details]. The slower rider usually takes [time] on a similar ride. We’re leaving at 8.30 am and need to be back by noon. Allow 25 minutes for photos and 20 minutes of spare time. What return time should we expect for each route?

Before setting off, check the access information for the Ma-2210. A notice may apply to particular dates, a section of road or a type of vehicle. If you’re planning next season’s holiday, check that the information covers the year of your trip and read the part about bicycles. Formentor access information and official DGT traffic notices

Where should you ride after picking up your bike?

Cala Sant Vicenç makes a good first afternoon ride from Port de Pollença. The official route follows paved roads for 14.96 km there and back, with about 137 m of climbing. At Cala Barques, you can stop by the water and look across at the rocky ridge of Cavall Bernat. It’s a good destination when you’ve spent the morning travelling and still want to see something of the island. Official cycling route to Cala Sant Vicenç

A short first ride also gives you time to settle into a rental bike. The saddle may feel right in the shop, then ten minutes later you notice you’re sitting a little differently than you do at home. Try the gears, settle your hands on the hoods and check that you can comfortably reach the brake levers. It’s easier to make a small adjustment today than to discover you need one halfway to Formentor tomorrow.

When you save the route, select road cycling in your planner and zoom in on the stretch between the port and Cala Sant Vicenç. Compare it with the paved route in the official guide. If the planner suggests a shortcut marked as a footpath or shows an unknown surface, adjust the route before sending it to your bike computer.

Four days of cycling in Port de Pollença

Say you arrive on the first morning and fly home on the fourth evening. You both want to see the coast, but only one of you wants to spend half a day on the bike. Here’s one way to plan the trip together.

Day 1. Pick up your bikes and ride to Cala Sant Vicenç

Allow time for luggage, your transfer and check-in when booking your bike pickup. Once you’re settled, ride to Cala Sant Vicenç and stop by the water. If your flight arrives late, a short spin around the port will give you time to check the saddle height and get comfortable before dinner.

Day 2. Ride to Cap de Formentor

Ride together to Mirador es Colomer and stop for the view. If you both have time and feel like riding further, carry on to the lighthouse. Save both routes the evening before, then check the wind forecast and road notices in the morning. If you plan to split up at the viewpoint, agree on a meeting place and time before you leave.

Day 3. Explore Pollença on foot

Spend the morning wandering through Pollença’s old town and stay for lunch. If today has the better forecast for the Formentor ride, swap days two and three. Keeping one morning flexible makes that an easy decision.

Day 4. One last coffee ride

Follow the coast from Port de Pollença towards Alcúdia, stop for coffee and ride back the same way. Work backwards from your airport transfer. Allow time to return the bikes, shower, collect your bags and reach the pickup point. What’s left is the time you can spend enjoying the ride.

If you’re travelling with someone who doesn’t cycle, start with something you want to do together. That could be breakfast in the port after your ride or lunch in the old town. Give ChatGPT that meeting time when you ask for a route. It’s a simple way to work out whether you have time for one more stop.

How can AI help you choose accommodation and work out the budget?

An apartment with a lower nightly rate can be awkward if every ride you want to do starts in Port de Pollença. To compare two places, give AI the location, full price and check-in time for each. Add your bike pickup address and transfer quotes for the number of people travelling. You can then compare the cost per person and see how much of your first afternoon you’ll have for riding.

Try this prompt when comparing two places to stay

Two of us are staying for three nights. Apartment A is in [location] and costs [price] for the whole stay. Apartment B is in [location] and costs [price]. Our return airport transfer would cost [price for A] or [price for B] for both of us. Two rental bikes cost [total price] for the full stay. Work out the total and the cost per person for each option. Flag any missing costs. Then write a short message in Spanish asking the host if there’s a lockable space for two bikes and whether we can leave our bags after check-out.

The answer about luggage can make a difference on your last day. If you can leave your bags after checking out, you may have time for a final ride. If you’re bringing your own bikes, include the number of bike cases when asking for a transfer quote. If you’re renting locally, check how the arrival time fits with your bike collection.

How do you find a road bike to rent in Port de Pollença?

Give the assistant your town, dates, height and the kind of riding you have in mind. If you want an electric road bike, say “electric road bike with drop handlebars”. A general search for an e-bike can bring up several types of bike with very different riding positions.

Try this prompt to find a bike for your dates

I’m looking to rent a road bike in Port de Pollença from [date] to [date]. I’m [height] cm tall. At home I ride a [model and size] and use [pedal system] pedals. Find options near [hotel or address]. Give me the pickup address, the booking link and the price for the whole rental period where available. Tell me whether my size is available for those exact dates. If the shop needs to confirm anything, draft a short message in English.

How do you get the route onto your bike computer?

Once you’ve chosen a ride, open your usual route planner, such as Komoot. Select road cycling, enter your exact start and add the places you want to visit in order. For a return ride, make the hotel or bike shop your final point as well as your start.

  • Check the full distance, elevation gain and surface. Use your own pace on similar terrain to estimate riding time, then add stops.
  • Save the full route and the shorter option under names you can recognise on a small screen, such as “Formentor lighthouse” and “Formentor viewpoint”.
  • Sync both routes to your bike computer, or download the GPX files if you usually import routes manually.
  • Open each route on the device before leaving your accommodation. Check the first turn, the finish and the total distance. A route that ends at the lighthouse only covers half of your day.

What if the weather changes overnight?

Check AEMET’s hourly forecast for Pollença. Look at the wind during the hours you expect to be on the peninsula and at the timing of any rain. Conditions on an exposed stretch of road can differ from those in town. With the latest forecast in front of you, use AI to swap rides around the commitments you already have. Try this prompt the evening before your ride

Here’s the latest forecast for the next two mornings: [paste forecast]. We have routes saved for Cala Sant Vicenç and Cap de Formentor, and we’d also like a morning to explore Pollença on foot. Tomorrow we’ve booked lunch at 1 pm. The following day we have no fixed return time. How would you arrange the two mornings? Tell us which details to check again before we set off.

The best shorter ride is one you’d happily choose anyway. That might be coffee overlooking Cala Barques or a morning at Mirador es Colomer with time to take in the view. Pick the place you most want to see, check the route from your own doorstep and leave a little room in the day to enjoy Mallorca.
