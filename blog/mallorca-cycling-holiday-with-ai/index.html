How to Plan a Cycling Holiday in Mallorca with AI

24 November 2026 · 11 min read

# Cycling in Mallorca with AI\. How to plan your trip, choose a route and rent a bike in Port de Pollença

You have four days in Mallorca\. You want to ride to the lighthouse at Cap de Formentor, stop for photos along the way and have lunch with your family\. You ask ChatGPT to plan a scenic bike ride in Mallorca\. It gives you a list that includes Formentor, Sóller and Sa Calobra\. They all sound tempting\. Which one actually fits into your morning?

At Venga Cycling Mallorca, we’d start with two questions\. Where are you riding from, and what time do you want to be back? Then we’d open the map\. Here’s how to go from your first chat with AI to a route you can follow on your bike computer\. We’ll use a four\-day stay in Port de Pollença, on Mallorca’s north coast, with time for three different rides and one morning off the bike\.

What should you tell AI to get a route that suits you?

Start with what you ride at home\. “I ride 60 km on flat roads at weekends, but I rarely descend on tight hairpins” gives an assistant more to work with than “I’m pretty fit”\. If there are two of you, describe both riders\. One of you might be looking forward to the climb, while the other is there for coffee by the sea\. There’s room for both in the same ride\.

Give your starting town or address\. Pollença and Port de Pollença are two different places\. Choose the wrong one and your door\-to\-door distance changes\. Add your dates, bike type, preferred road surface and the time you need to be back\. If you’re free from 9:00 am to noon and like to stop for half an hour, you have at most two and a half hours of riding time\.

Copy this into ChatGPT and fill in your details

We’re spending four days in Port de Pollença on [dates]. We ride road bikes. I usually ride 70 km, and the other rider usually does 40 km on flat roads. We want to ride together, see the sea and try one longer climb. We have two mornings of three hours each and one morning with no fixed return time. Suggest an itinerary with one day off the bike. For each ride, give us a destination, a turnaround point and time for stops. Link to your sources for the distances. If you need more information, ask me two questions first.

How do you plan a ride to Cap de Formentor?

First, decide how far along Formentor you want to ride\. You could turn around at the Mirador es Colomer viewpoint, ride to Platja de Formentor beach or continue to the lighthouse at the end of the peninsula\. Each makes for a different ride\. If you want a photo at the lighthouse, enter Far de Formentor in your route planner and add the return leg to where you’re staying\.

The official Pollença route guide lists 40\.5 km round trip from Port de Pollença to the lighthouse via Platja de Formentor and rates the route as difficult\. If 40 km sounds like a quick spin after work, keep the return leg in mind\. You still have rolling terrain to cover after your stop at the lighthouse\. Some of the descents you enjoyed on the way out will be climbs on the way back\. Pollença’s official route guide to the lighthouse

Say you’re meeting your family for lunch at 12:30 pm and want to be back at the hotel by noon\. Save two routes from the same starting point\. One goes to Mirador es Colomer and back\. The other goes all the way to the lighthouse\. If the full ride only fits your morning once you take out all the stops, choose the viewpoint\. Getting off the bike for a few minutes is part of the ride to Formentor\.

I have two routes starting at the same hotel. Here are the distance, elevation gain and estimated riding time for each [paste data from the route planner]. We’re leaving at 8:30 am and want to be back at the hotel by noon. Allow 25 minutes for photos and 20 minutes as a buffer. Calculate how long each option will take and how much time we’ll have left before noon. Use the slower rider’s pace.

Before you set off, check the access rules for the Ma\-2210 road\. Notices can apply to a specific stretch of road, dates and types of vehicles\. If you’re planning for next season, look for information covering the year of your trip\. If you see a headline about car restrictions, open the notice and check what it says about cycling to the lighthouse on your dates\. Formentor access information · Traffic notices from Spain’s DGT

Where should you ride after picking up your bike?

Cala Sant Vicenç is a good first ride from Port de Pollença\. Pollença’s official guide describes a paved 14\.96 km round trip with about 137 m of climbing\. From Cala Barques, you can see the distinctive rocky ridge of Cavall Bernat\. It gives you somewhere worth riding to on your first afternoon, even if your flight leaves you with little time\. Route guide to Cala Sant Vicenç

A short ride also gives you a chance to check your bike setup\. The saddle might feel right in the shop, then a few minutes into the ride you notice you’re sitting differently than you do at home\. Check the shifting, where your hands naturally rest and whether you can comfortably reach the brake levers\. If anything needs adjusting, it’s easier to sort it out before your morning in the hills\.

Set the bay as your destination and select the road cycling profile in your route planner\. Zoom in on the stretch between Port de Pollença and Cala Sant Vicenç, and compare it with the paved route in the official guide\. The shortest line between the two bays might look fine on your phone\. A closer look will show whether any sections are marked as footpaths or have an unknown surface\.

Say you arrive on the first morning, leave on the fourth evening and only one of you wants to spend half a day on the bike\. Here’s one way to plan the trip together\.

If you’re travelling with someone who doesn’t ride, start with something you want to do together\. That might be breakfast in the port after your ride or lunch in the old town\. Give AI a specific return time to plan around\. On the ride, you’ll have an easier time deciding whether there’s still half an hour for coffee\.

How do you work out the budget and choose where to stay?

A low\-priced apartment elsewhere on the island can be inconvenient if you want to start every ride in Port de Pollença\. To compare two places, give AI their locations, total prices and check\-in times\. Add the bike rental address and a transfer quote for your group\. Ask it to calculate the cost per person and estimate how long you’ll need between landing and picking up your bikes\.

There are two of us staying for three nights. Accommodation A costs […] for the whole stay and accommodation B costs […]. A one-way transfer for two costs […] to A and […] to B. Two bikes for the whole stay cost […] in total. Work out the total and the price per person for each option. Flag any costs I haven’t included. Write a short message in Spanish asking the host if there’s a lockable space for two bikes and whether we can collect our luggage after check-out.

On the last day, the host’s answer may determine whether you have time for one more ride\. Check the transfer details too\. If you’re bringing your own bikes, specify how many bike cases you’ll have when you ask for a quote\. If you’re renting locally, check that your transfer schedule leaves enough time to pick up the bikes and get them set up\.

How do you find bike rental in Mallorca with AI?

If you ask “Where can I rent a bike in Mallorca?”, the assistant has the whole island to choose from\. Give it the town, your dates and what you ride\. If you’re looking for an electric road bike, say “electric road bike with drop handlebars”\. A search for “e\-bike” can bring up several other kinds of bikes\.

I’m looking for road bike rental in Port de Pollença for [dates]. I’m [height] cm tall. At home I ride a [model and size], and I use [pedal system] pedals. Compare rental options near [accommodation address]. Give me the pickup address, a booking link and the price for the full rental period if the current listing provides it. How do you get an AI\-suggested route onto your bike computer?

1. Open your usual route planner, such as Komoot\. Select road cycling and mark the exact start, waypoints and finish\. On an out\-and\-back ride, the finish will be your hotel or the bike shop\. 2. Compare the full distance, elevation gain and road surface with the route description you used to make the plan\. If AI estimates two hours and you know your pace on similar terrain, use your own estimate and add time for stops\. 3. Save the full route and the shorter option with clear names, such as “Formentor lighthouse” and “Formentor viewpoint”\. Sync them to your bike computer using a method it supports, or download a GPX file if you import routes manually\. 4. Open the route on your device before you leave\. Check the first turn, the finish and the total distance\. It’s easy to overlook a saved route that ends at the lighthouse when your morning plan also includes the ride back\.

What if the weather changes in the morning?

Check AEMET’s hourly forecast for Pollença\. Look at the wind during the hours you’ll be on the peninsula and when rain is expected\. Conditions on an exposed stretch of road can differ from the forecast for town\. Paste the latest forecast into your chat and ask AI how to rearrange the days you’ve already planned\. AEMET’s hourly forecast for Pollença

Here’s the forecast for the next two mornings […]. We’ve saved rides to Cala Sant Vicenç and Formentor and have a free day in Pollença. We’ve booked lunch tomorrow at 1:00 pm and have more time the day after. How should we rearrange our plans? Include the option of going for a walk and tell us what to check in the morning.

A good trip plan gives you a shorter option you’ll look forward to as well\. It could be your first coffee overlooking Cala Barques or a morning at Mirador es Colomer with time for photos\. Pick one place you really want to see, check the route from your doorstep and leave yourself time to enjoy Mallorca\.
