Cycling in Mallorca Off-Season: Bike Rental & Hotel Delivery

27 October 2026 · 4 min read

The peak cycling season in Mallorca is slowly coming to an end. The roads are getting quieter, the number of tourists is decreasing, and the island is returning to its more relaxed rhythm.

But does that mean the cycling season is over?

Absolutely not!

At Venga Cycling Mallorca, we're still operating at full speed, helping cyclists discover the beauty of Mallorca throughout autumn and winter.

In fact, visiting Mallorca outside the peak season might be one of the best decisions you make for your next cycling holiday.

Why Visit Mallorca for Cycling in the Off-Season?

1. Cheaper Flights and More Affordable Travel

One of the biggest advantages of travelling to Mallorca outside the high season is the opportunity to find significantly cheaper flights.

As tourist demand decreases, airlines often offer more attractive fares, while hotels and holiday accommodation may also become more affordable.

This makes autumn and winter excellent opportunities to organise a cycling training camp, a spontaneous weekend getaway, or a longer cycling holiday without the higher costs of peak-season travel.

Less spending on travel means more opportunities to enjoy Mallorca.

2. Great Cycling Temperatures Even in Autumn and Winter

While much of Europe is already experiencing cold and rainy weather, Mallorca continues to offer a relatively mild Mediterranean climate.

Average daytime high temperatures around Palma are approximately:

  • October: 24°C
  • November: 19°C
  • December: 16°C

Of course, autumn and winter can bring occasional rain and wind, but many days remain suitable for cycling.

For cyclists who prefer comfortable temperatures instead of intense summer heat, this can be an ideal time to explore the island.

3. Quieter Roads and a More Authentic Mallorca

Imagine riding through the beautiful Serra de Tramuntana mountains, enjoying the famous climbs of Coll de Femenia, or discovering the spectacular coastal scenery around Cap de Formentor.

Now imagine doing it with fewer tourists and less traffic.

That's one of the biggest advantages of cycling in Mallorca outside the peak season.

The island feels calmer, the cafés are less crowded, and there's more time to enjoy every kilometre.

Whether you're training seriously or simply exploring Mallorca at your own pace, the off-season offers a completely different cycling experience.

Bike Delivery to Hotels Across Mallorca

We understand that not every cyclist visiting Mallorca stays in Port de Pollença.

That's why Venga Cycling Mallorca also offers bike delivery to hotels in different parts of the island, subject to prior arrangement.

If you're staying in a hotel or other accommodation and would prefer to have your rental bike delivered directly to your location, simply contact us before your arrival.

Let us know:

  • Your rental dates
  • The name and location of your hotel
  • The type of bike you would like to rent
  • Your preferred delivery arrangements

We'll check availability and discuss the delivery options and conditions with you.

Please note: Hotel bike delivery must be arranged in advance and is subject to availability, location, and individually agreed conditions.

Our aim is to make your cycling holiday as convenient as possible, so you can spend less time organising logistics and more time enjoying the roads of Mallorca.
