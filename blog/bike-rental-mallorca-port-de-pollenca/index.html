Bike Rental in Mallorca: Why Port de Pollença Is the Perfect Starting Point

5 October 2026 · 5 min read

Mallorca is one of Europe's most popular cycling destinations. But choosing the right place to stay — and the right place to rent your bike — can make a huge difference to your cycling holiday.

If you are looking for bike rental in Mallorca, Port de Pollença is one of the best places to start.

Located in the north of the island, it gives you direct access to quiet country roads, coastal routes and some of Mallorca's most famous climbs. You can leave your hotel, pick up your bike and start riding without spending the morning in a car.

At Venga Cycling Mallorca, this is exactly why we chose Port de Pollença as our base.

Why rent a bike in Port de Pollença?

Location is one of the biggest advantages.

From Port de Pollença you can ride towards the Tramuntana mountains, explore the flatter roads around Pollença, Sa Pobla and Muro, or follow the coast towards Alcúdia.

For stronger riders, Port de Pollença is also an excellent starting point for some of Mallorca's legendary cycling destinations, including Coll de Femenia and Sa Calobra.

This means you don't need a car or transfer to enjoy great road cycling in Mallorca.

Pick up your bike, fill your bottles and ride.

Road bike rental in Mallorca: choose the bike for your ride

Not every cyclist visiting Mallorca has the same plans.

Some riders want comfortable days exploring the island. Others arrive with one objective: mountains, kilometres and serious training.

That is why choosing the right road bike matters.

At Venga Cycling Mallorca we offer different levels of road bikes, from versatile carbon road bikes to premium performance machines.

A bike such as the Giant TCR is an excellent choice if you want a balanced road bike for long days, climbing and exploring Mallorca.

For riders looking for something faster and more performance-focused, the Giant Propel offers an aerodynamic platform designed for speed.

We also offer options for riders looking for electric road bikes, gravel bikes and high-performance cycling equipment.

Your rental bike should fit you

One of the most common questions we receive is:

What bike size do I need?

Height is a useful starting point, but it isn't the whole story.

Your riding position, saddle height, reach and personal preferences can significantly change how comfortable you feel after several hours on the bike.

If you already have bike fitting measurements from your own bike, send them to us before your arrival.

Whenever possible, we can prepare your rental bike using those measurements.

You can also bring your own saddle, pedals or bike computer mount if you prefer to ride with equipment you already know.

The objective is simple: spend less time adjusting the bike and more time riding Mallorca.

What is included with your bike rental?

When you rent a road bike, the small details matter.

Our bikes are carefully prepared before collection and include a saddle bag with an inner tube and tyre levers.

Garmin, Wahoo and Giant computer mounts are available, and pedals can also be added to your reservation.

Helmets are available for riders who prefer not to travel with their own.

Before leaving the shop, we can help you make final adjustments so that the bike is ready for your first ride.

Do you need to bring your own bike to Mallorca?

Not necessarily.

Travelling with your own bike means packing it correctly, transporting a large bike case to the airport and organising transport after landing in Mallorca.

For many cyclists, renting a quality road bike locally is simply easier.

Book your bike before your trip, arrive in Port de Pollença and collect a bike that has already been prepared for you.

It also gives you an opportunity to ride a different bike from the one you use at home.

Cycling routes from Port de Pollença

Another reason to choose a bike rental in Port de Pollença is the variety of routes available directly from town.

For an easier day, you can ride towards Cala Sant Vicenç or explore the flatter roads connecting Pollença, Sa Pobla, Muro and Alcúdia.

If you want some climbing, head towards Coll de Femenia and the Tramuntana mountains.

And if you are ready for one of Mallorca's iconic cycling experiences, continue towards Sa Calobra.

There are options for short recovery rides, long endurance days and serious mountain training — all starting from the same area.

When should you book a road bike in Mallorca?

Mallorca's main cycling seasons attract thousands of riders, particularly during spring and autumn.

If you are travelling during a busy cycling period, we recommend booking your bike in advance.

This is particularly important if you need a specific frame size or want a premium road bike.

Booking early also gives us more time to prepare your setup before you arrive.

Bike rental for cycling groups in Mallorca

Travelling with a cycling club or a group of friends?

Organising bikes for a larger group requires more planning than an individual rental.

Different heights, bike sizes, pedals and riding preferences all need to be considered.

At Venga Cycling Mallorca we can prepare bikes for groups and help coordinate the rental before arrival.

For groups of more than eight riders, we also offer a group discount.

Contact us before your trip with the number of riders, dates and approximate heights and we can check availability.
