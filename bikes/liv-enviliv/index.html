Who it is for

Liv's women-specific aero race bike — the counterpart of the Propel, built around women's fit data. For fast riders who want race-level speed on Mallorca's flatter and rolling roads.

Specification

Groupset and equipment: Shimano Dura-Ace Di2 · CADEX wheels · Power meter. Each bike comes prepared with a saddle bag, spare inner tube, tyre levers and a pump. Computer mounts for Garmin, Wahoo and Giant are free; pedals and helmets can be added when you book.
