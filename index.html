The people behind it

More than a place.

Venga has its home in Mallorca, but its story began years earlier in Poland — at races, training camps and over thousands of kilometres ridden together. Patryk and Kacper first met as young riders competing against each other.

"Szczepan Bike became our second family."

The idea behind Venga grew naturally: a place shaped by how cyclists actually ride and travel — great bikes, local knowledge, personal support and, above all, the right people.
